# Wedding Wrapped

Per-guest photo recaps from Preethi & Neel's wedding (Apr 24–25, 2026), delivered privately to each guest.

## People

**Guest**:
A person on the guest list who attended at least one event.
_Avoid_: Attendee, user, invitee

**Household**:
The guests who share one `party` on the guest list; one Card and one Login email go to a household.
_Avoid_: Family, party, group

## What guests see

**Deck**:
One Household's recap page of curated photos; a one-person Household's Deck is just that guest's.
_Avoid_: Wrapped page, recap, profile, family page, Hub

**Selection**:
The members of a Household a Deck is currently showing: any combination, never empty. All members selected is the whole family.
_Avoid_: Filter, personal deck, tab, profile, "Everyone" view

**Link**:
The private, unguessable URL of a Deck. One per Household. Holding it is the only access control.
_Avoid_: Token URL, magic link

## Getting to it

**Card**:
The printed thank-you card mailed to a Household; identical for everyone except the handwritten name, and it points to Login.
_Avoid_: Thank-you note, invite

**Login**:
The page where a guest enters their email to be sent their Household's Link. It does not sign anyone in.
_Avoid_: Sign-in, auth, magic-link login

**Login email**:
The email Login sends: one button to the Household's Deck.
_Avoid_: Magic link email, send email

**Send**:
The one-time outbound email to every Household announcing the Decks.
_Avoid_: Blast, campaign
