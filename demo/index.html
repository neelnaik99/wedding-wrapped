How it works

From one gallery to 72 households

  1. Find every face. A face model reads each photo and turns every face into a numeric fingerprint.
  2. Group faces into people. Similar fingerprints are clustered, and an admin tool lets a person name each cluster and fix mistakes.
  3. Score every shot. Eyes open, smiling, sharp, and in focus or in the background. Each guest gets their own quality bar, because lighting differs by event.
  4. Pick the highlights. About 25 per guest, one per scene, split between photos with the couple, solo shots and group shots.
  5. Build one page per household. A member picker drives every section, and friend groups found from who appears together become an interactive map.
  6. Check, then ship. Privacy rules are re-checked on every deploy. Guests type their email on the site and get their household's link.
Privacy rules

What each guest can see

Only your household

A page holds only photos that include someone in the household, or just the couple. Other photos are never shipped, so there is nothing to unhide.

Never your worst shots

You only see photos where you clear your own quality bar. Photos where someone else looks bad are pushed down and capped.

Fails closed

A deploy stops if any photo breaks the household rule, or if any public file contains an email, a guest ID or another guest's link.
